# IRAC

Trigger sentences are fully self-contained — do NOT paraphrase, abbreviate, or substitute variables.

## Trigger Sentences

**Software**:
> For each significant design choice in this plan, state the engineering principle or constraint it should satisfy, analyse whether the plan meets it, and conclude with a clear pass/fail/insufficient-information verdict.

**Business**:
> For each significant strategic choice in this plan, state the business principle or market constraint it should satisfy, analyse whether the plan meets it, and conclude with a clear pass/fail/insufficient-information verdict.

**Research**:
> For each significant methodological choice in this study design, state the scientific principle or validity criterion it should satisfy, analyse whether the design meets it, and conclude with a clear pass/fail/insufficient-information verdict.

**Education**:
> For each significant pedagogical choice in this plan, state the learning science principle or assessment standard it should satisfy, analyse whether the plan meets it, and conclude with a clear pass/fail/insufficient-information verdict.

**Policy**:
> For each significant regulatory or governance choice in this plan, state the legal principle or policy standard it should satisfy, analyse whether the plan meets it, and conclude with a clear pass/fail/insufficient-information verdict.

**Creative**:
> For each significant craft choice in this plan, state the aesthetic principle or medium convention it should satisfy, analyse whether the plan meets it, and conclude with a clear pass/fail/insufficient-information verdict.

## Artefact Format

```markdown
## Grounding Report (IRAC)

### Issue 1: [Design/Strategic Choice]

**Rule**: [Principle or constraint that governs this choice]
**Application**: [Analysis of whether the plan satisfies the principle]
**Conclusion**: **Pass** / **Fail** / **Insufficient Information**
[If fail: what specifically violates the principle]
[If insufficient: what information is needed to evaluate]

### Issue 2: [Design/Strategic Choice]
...
```
