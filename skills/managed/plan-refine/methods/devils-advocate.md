# Devil's Advocate

Trigger sentences are fully self-contained — do NOT paraphrase, abbreviate, or substitute variables.

## Trigger Sentences

**Software**:
> Play devil's advocate: identify the single weakest design decision in this plan, explain why it will cause the most damage, and propose a specific alternative — do not list multiple concerns, commit to one.

**Business**:
> Play devil's advocate: identify the single weakest strategic assumption in this plan, explain why it will cause the most damage to the business, and propose a specific alternative — do not list multiple concerns, commit to one.

**Research**:
> Play devil's advocate: identify the single weakest methodological choice in this study design, explain why it will most undermine the validity of results, and propose a specific alternative — do not list multiple concerns, commit to one.

**Education**:
> Play devil's advocate: identify the single weakest pedagogical assumption in this plan, explain why it will most undermine learning outcomes, and propose a specific alternative — do not list multiple concerns, commit to one.

**Policy**:
> Play devil's advocate: identify the single weakest assumption in this policy, explain why it will cause the most harm to affected populations, and propose a specific alternative — do not list multiple concerns, commit to one.

**Creative**:
> Play devil's advocate: identify the single weakest creative decision in this plan, explain why it will most alienate the target audience, and propose a specific alternative — do not list multiple concerns, commit to one.

## Artefact Format

```markdown
## Risk Report (Devil's Advocate)

### Weakest Decision

**Decision**: [the specific choice being challenged]
**Location**: [section/heading in plan]

### Why This Fails

[Causal argument: why this specific decision will cause the most damage]

### Proposed Alternative

[Specific replacement with rationale]
```
