# Laddering Probe Templates

Use these probe templates during Phase 4 (Laddering Probes) to dig deeper into persona motivations.

## Core Probes (Use These First)

### Why Probes (Drilling Down)
| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 왜 이걸 선택했어요? | Why did you choose this? | After any decision |
| 뭐가 끌렸어요? | What drew you to it? | When choice seems positive |
| 뭐가 싫었어요? | What put you off? | When choice seems avoidant |
| 그게 왜 중요해요? | Why does that matter? | To go one level deeper |

### Consequence Probes (Exploring Impact)
| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 그래서 뭐가 좋아요? | So what's good about that? | After positive reasoning |
| 그래서 뭐가 문제예요? | So what's the problem? | After negative reasoning |
| 그게 되면 어떨 것 같아요? | How would it feel if that worked? | To access emotional payoff |
| 그게 안 되면요? | What if that doesn't work? | To access fear/avoidance |

### Alternative Probes (Testing Flexibility)
| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 이게 없었으면 뭘 했을까요? | What would you do if this wasn't an option? | To find second-choice patterns |
| 다른 선택지가 있었다면? | What if there were other options? | To surface unmet needs |
| 반대로 했으면 어땠을까요? | What if you'd done the opposite? | To explore counterfactuals |

## Specialized Probes

### Engagement Probes
| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 이 순간 계속하고 싶었어요? | Did you want to continue at this moment? | To find pull/push |
| 여기서 그만둘 수도 있었나요? | Could you have stopped here? | To find drop points |
| 뭐가 궁금했어요? | What were you curious about? | To surface active questions |
| 다음에 뭘 기대했어요? | What did you expect next? | To find expectation gaps |

### Emotional Probes
| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 이때 기분이 어땠어요? | How did you feel at this moment? | After key events |
| 뭔가 불편했어요? | Was something uncomfortable? | When sensing resistance |
| 설레거나 두근거렸어요? | Were you excited or nervous? | When sensing engagement |
| 지루했던 부분이 있었어요? | Was any part boring? | To find dead zones |

### Expectation Probes
| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 뭘 기대했는데 안 나왔어요? | What did you expect that didn't happen? | To find gaps |
| 예상 못 한 게 있었어요? | Was there anything unexpected? | To find surprises |
| 이게 뭐라고 생각했어요? | What did you think this was? | To check mental model |

## Laddering Sequences

### The Value Ladder (Why → Why → Why)
Start with behavior, keep asking why until you hit a core value:

```
Q: 왜 문을 안 열었어요?
A: 무섭게 느껴져서요.

Q: 뭐가 무서웠어요?
A: 뭐가 나올지 모르니까요.

Q: 모르는 게 왜 무서워요?
A: 통제할 수 없으니까요. → [Core value: control]
```

### The Consequence Ladder (So What → So What)
Start with a stated preference, explore its implications:

```
Q: 왜 이 선택지가 좋았어요?
A: 더 안전해 보여서요.

Q: 안전하면 뭐가 좋아요?
A: 실수 안 하니까요.

Q: 실수 안 하면 뭐가 좋아요?
A: 바보처럼 안 보이니까요. → [Core value: competence/image]
```

## When to Stop Laddering

Stop when you reach:
1. **A terminal value**: Freedom, security, belonging, competence, pleasure
2. **Circular answers**: They start repeating themselves
3. **Discomfort**: The persona would realistically deflect
4. **Insight saturation**: You've learned enough to inform design

## Game-Specific Probes (from Gary Klein)

These probes capture cognitive phenomena unique to game experiences.

### Phantom Option Probe
Surfaces the gap between player intent and what the game allows.

| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 게임이 시켜주지 않는 건데 하고 싶은 게 있었어요? | Was there something you wanted to do that the game wouldn't let you? | After constrained choices |
| 다른 방법으로 하고 싶었어요? | Did you want to approach this differently? | When choice feels forced |
| 없는 선택지를 찾았어요? | Were you looking for an option that wasn't there? | At decision points |

**Why this matters**: If the answer is rich, the game is failing to support player intent. Design opportunity.

### Wonder Probe
Tracks whether curiosity is alive or dead.

| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 지금 이 순간 뭐가 궁금해요? | What are you curious about right now? | Every 2 turns |
| 알고 싶은 게 생겼어요? | Did something make you want to know more? | After new information |
| 그 궁금증이 해결됐어요? | Was that curiosity satisfied? | After plot reveals |

**Warning sign**: If "뭐가 궁금해요?" yields "별로요" (nothing much), the game's curiosity engine is dead.

### Cue Probe
Pushes past surface rationalization to find actual perceptual triggers.

| Korean | English | When to use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 어떤 글자/색/위치가 그렇게 느끼게 했어요? | What text/color/position made you feel that way? | After surface "why" answer |
| 화면에서 뭐가 먼저 눈에 들어왔어요? | What caught your eye first on the screen? | At any decision point |
| 그게 [X]라고 느껴진 건 구체적으로 뭐 때문이에요? | What specifically made it feel like [X]? | When answer is vague |

**Klein's warning**: Players aren't lying when they say "재밌어서요" — they're just unaware of the subtle cues that actually drove the decision. If you don't find the Cue, you can't design the fix.

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Leading questions**: "그게 좋았죠?" → Use "그게 어땠어요?" instead
- **Too many whys**: 5+ in a row feels like interrogation
- **Ignoring deflection**: If persona would change subject, honor that
- **Forcing depth**: Some choices are genuinely surface-level
- **Accepting first Why**: Push past "재밌어서요" to find the actual cue
