# Personal work, Secretariat, and AI services

Personal material, AI work records, scheduled execution, and reader-facing services serve different purposes and access boundaries. Menus and available capabilities depend on your role, installation version, and server settings.

## Personal Space and My Todo

Personal Space preserves your own material separately from shared Project files. Delivery to another Project requires an identified destination and access checks.

**My Todo** is your private checklist. Add items and mark completion through its available controls. When asking AI to register a personal task, explicitly say “Add this to My Todo.”

| Surface | Purpose |
|---|---|
| My Todo | Your private checklist |
| Work Steps | Steps and state of the current AI run |
| My Work | Related work records, execution evidence, and conversations |
| Automations | Scheduled or recurring work |

A checklist item does not itself create a scheduled job. Likewise, AI progress cards do not automatically become your personal tasks.

## Secretariat

Where enabled, Secretariat is an account-owned space for personal assistance, conversation, briefings, and proposals related to ongoing work. Its availability and detailed behavior depend on the server's migrations and runtime configuration.

Review evidence and decide which proposals to act on. A proposal or briefing is not proof that the suggested work has executed. Personal assistance does not automatically authorize sending messages externally, changing shared files, or granting access.

Only context within the current account's applicable access scope should be used. Do not treat Secretariat as access to every user's private Sessions. If an entry cannot open or its conversation cannot load, check with your administrator instead of assuming all related backend capabilities are enabled.

## People and AI connections

A people or AI connection describes participation and access relationships. It is different from a **service Connection**, which lets an Agent use an authorized external account or API.

Joining a collaboration surface or seeing an AI teammate card does not automatically grant access to every file, tool, or credential that teammate uses. Follow the access controls shown for the specific operation.

## AI services and dashboards

An Agent workspace may expose registered dashboards or reader-facing AI services. Treat these as specific surfaces with their own audience and access policy, not as unrestricted Project access.

Saving or registering a dashboard is separate from publishing it, updating its underlying data, or starting a recurring job. Check the data's source and freshness. Do not assume a page is live, publicly available, or automatically refreshing simply because it appears in the workspace.

Ask explicitly for publication or an update schedule when needed; confirm its audience, scope, and service dependencies first.

## Experimental voice

Voice is opt-in at the deployment level and is not enabled on every installation. Available input, read-aloud, and service options also depend on provider configuration and browser support. Check microphone permissions and review dictated text before sending.

A development or administration Voice Lab screen does not prove that real-time voice conversation is available to ordinary users.
